✦ The Folder ✦

nine pockets to a page · pull one out

Turn the page, then pull a card out of its sleeve. It flies into the starfield and becomes a live 3D lens: the artwork carries its own relief, the border is raised geometry rather than a drawn line, and the layers parallax as it tilts. Move your pointer over it — or tilt the phone. Empty pockets are cards nobody has filed yet.
