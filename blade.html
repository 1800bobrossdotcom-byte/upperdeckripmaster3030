This game needs WebGL 2

THE LIGHT renders through PlayCanvas, which has no software fallback — so rather than show you a black rectangle, it says so.

If you are on an older browser, or on a machine with GPU acceleration switched off, that is the thing to change. The rest of the site works fine without it.
