What this is, and what it is not

A proposed landing surface, built to be argued with. The front page is still index.html and nothing here has replaced it.

Made of — warm card stock, three spot inks (acid, cyan, key) laid down at three registrations, and one hot-foil stamp in the tail margin. Ink multiplies the paper and never adds, which is why the stock is pale on a black site: on black stock there is nothing to print with.

Lit by — the two torches off the front page, promoted to real lamps: warm, raking, flickering. Phosphor green is the room, not an ink. Cyan is the rim that finds a torn edge.

What moves — nothing, until you touch it. Then the stock dishes under your finger, the perforation gives, and the sheet snaps back when the card comes away. The tear does not reverse: paper does not un-tear.

Not done — the card faces are generated placeholder ink, not the artist's work; the eight names are a first cut; and the whole page is disposable by design. Strike what is wrong. NFA — experimental art token, it can go to zero.
