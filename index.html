How it works
the actual facts · no corporate

1 The token

$3030 is the fuel — one ERC-20 on SuperRare Liquid Editions. Minted once (3,030,000), the whole supply in the pool on day one, no team pre-mint. Buy it and sell it back on the curve any time.

2 Rip a pack

Seven cards for about $10 of $3030, in one transaction: half burns and half funds the studio. The pack is priced in dollars ($10 → $20 across four tiers) and the token amount is fixed from the live price when a tier opens. Each tier's allotment dwindles.

3 The fire

Burned supply never comes back. ⚠ We publish no target burn percentage, and that is deliberate: a pack costs a fixed number of dollars, so a more expensive token burns fewer tokens — any figure printed here would go stale with nobody editing it. We show what has actually burned, read live off the chain, and the studio's total next to it. ⚠ That other half is revenue, and the studio's holding is the concentration risk here — not the burn. You should hear that from us.

4 Every card is a lens

All 100 cards are live renders that read the market and the fire. 33 mint as 1/1s — 11 auctioned on SuperRare, 11 pulled from packs, and 11 earned by clearing a named feat in the games, printed in full and impossible to buy. The other 67 are render-only. Nothing turns to ash; rarity is set by community vote. Every holder carries Lovebeing.

5 Play for the titles

Six games, in this browser. Stake your cards, wager $3030 — a small rake burns and the podium splits the rest — and your cards are not cosmetic: they arm you, scaled by live chain heat. Practice is open to everyone, wallet or no.

⚠ The house rule: on-chain there is exactly one token and one move — pay $3030. A pack and the game rake split half-and-half; conviction burns and compression burn outright. The studio's cut is stated on the tin. Nothing hidden, nothing extra. Court votes and wagers are site-side signal; the fire is the only consensus.
Today the pack, folder, arena & companion cards are a testnet prototype — pulls and card ownership save in your browser, and on-chain cards ship in a later phase. The one thing that is real right now: the $3030 buy & burn.

Where this is going

We want to put the games on Google Play. All six — THE CITY, RIP ROCKETER, CLOUD RACER, DOGFIGHT, SECTION 9 TASK FORCE and THE ARENA — already run in a browser; on a phone's home screen they would be played in the places a browser tab never gets opened — on a couch, on a train — and every pack ripped and every rake burned there is the same $3030 moving on the same chain. The point of the port is not distribution for its own sake: it is to put the on-chain half of this game in front of people who would never have gone looking for it.

And we want to print a physical deck. The trading card is a size before it is anything else — palm, phone, a front that shows and a back that tells — and this whole studio is built out of that shape. A real deck you can hold, riffle and sleeve is the form finally arriving in the form it was borrowed from.

Both are ambitions, not announcements — no date, no store listing, no print run. When either becomes real it will be shown, not promised.
