SET A PLATE

Eight lines of thirty-two cells. Two hundred and fifty-six bytes, the ABI word as the measure. Type to ink a cell; the arrow keys move the carriage without inking. A cell you leave alone is paper. A space you type is paid for, and leaves a mark.

The picture is drawn from the bytes alone — no lighting, no foil, nothing that moves. The die is a fixed 2:3 card, so the ragged edge is a margin and never the outline of the object.

WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT DO

It does not press. The contract does not exist yet, on purpose: the one thing this design turns on — whether a plate reads as a page or as a chart — cannot be settled on a screen, so nothing gets written in Solidity until plates have been printed at card size and looked at on a bench. There is no wallet on this page, nothing to sign and nothing to approve. Compose a plate, keep the link, and it will still be exactly this plate later.
